Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Data Collection and Usage

LinguaLink is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when using our speech translation service.

Audio Data Processing

  • Voice Recordings: We temporarily process your voice recordings solely for translation purposes
  • Real-time Processing: Audio data is processed in real-time and is not stored permanently
  • No Personal Storage: We do not retain personal voice recordings after translation
  • Secure Transmission: All audio data is transmitted securely using industry-standard encryption

Third-Party Services

We use Deepgram Nova-2 for advanced speech recognition. Deepgram's privacy policy governs their processing of audio data for transcription services.

Data Retention

  • Voice recordings are processed in real-time and deleted immediately after translation
  • Translation logs may be kept for service improvement (anonymized)
  • No personal identifiers are stored with usage data

User Rights

  • Consent: By using this service, you consent to audio processing for translation
  • Opt-out: You can stop using the service at any time
  • Data Deletion: Contact us to request deletion of any stored data

Security Measures

We implement industry-standard security measures including encryption in transit, secure API connections, and minimal data retention policies.

Google Play Store Compliance

This privacy policy complies with Google Play Store requirements for apps that process audio data. We are transparent about data collection and provide clear user consent mechanisms.

Contact Information

For privacy-related questions or data requests, please contact us through the app or visit our website.
