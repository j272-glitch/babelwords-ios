Steps for Android Deep Links Configuration
1. Verify assetlinks.json is Accessible
Your assetlinks.json is already configured at public/.well-known/assetlinks.json. Ensure it's accessible at:

https://linguagt.com/.well-known/assetlinks.json
The current configuration looks correct for package com.lingualink.test with your SHA-256 fingerprint.

2. Update Capacitor Configuration for Android Deep Links
capacitor.config.ts
Add Android deep linking configuration
3. Sync assetlinks.json Package Name
Your assetlinks.json already uses com.lingualink.test - ensure this matches across all Android configurations.

4. Add Intent Filters to AndroidManifest.xml
When building your Android app, ensure the AndroidManifest.xml includes these intent filters:

<activity android:name=".MainActivity" android:exported="true">
    <!-- App Links for https://linguagt.com -->
    <intent-filter android:autoVerify="true">
        <action android:name="android.intent.action.VIEW" />
        <category android:name="android.intent.category.DEFAULT" />
        <category android:name="android.intent.category.BROWSABLE" />
        <data android:scheme="https" android:host="linguagt.com" />
    </intent-filter>
    
    <!-- Custom scheme fallback -->
    <intent-filter>
        <action android:name="android.intent.action.VIEW" />
        <category android:name="android.intent.category.DEFAULT" />
        <category android:name="android.intent.category.BROWSABLE" />
        <data android:scheme="linguagt" />
    </intent-filter>
</activity>
